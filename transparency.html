Transparency & live donation total

Where the Pro license money actually goes, and the paper trail that proves it.

Every Pro license payment for Reddit Picture Gallery Downloader is donated to CanKids...KidsCan, India's national society for childhood cancer care. You pay the developer on Ko-fi; the developer donates it onward monthly and posts the receipt publicly on the same Ko-fi page.

The honest version of "100%", because you should be able to check it rather than take it on faith: Ko-fi's payment processor takes its cut before the money ever reaches the developer's account. Every cent that does arrive is donated on — none of it is kept. Card processing carries a small percentage plus a flat per-transaction fee, so the flat part bites hardest on the $1 tier and barely registers on $10 — which is why the monthly receipts, not any worked example, are the number to hold this project to.
